Loop of the Recursive Dragon

A quiz-RPG for practicing course material. Battle monsters by answering questions.

How it works

Each question is an encounter with a monster. A right answer damages the monster; a wrong or incomplete answer lets it hit you back. Clear all the questions in a set and you finish the dungeon.

  • Anything you miss comes back. Missed questions return later in the run, so you cannot skip past something you do not understand.
  • A boss waits at the end if you stumbled on anything. It is made of exactly those questions, and it falls only when you have them right.
  • Mentors show up to teach. When a scene appears, read it — it demonstrates the technique the next few questions ask for.
  • If your health hits zero the run ends and that set starts over. Leveling up earns revive charges, and items drop as you defeat monsters.

How your grade works

Every set is worth the same share of your total. How much of that share a set pays out depends on the rank you have carried it to:

RankHow you earn itCredit
🥉 ApprenticeFinish the set once80%
🥈 JourneymanPass a rank trial, 3+ days after finishing90%
🥇 MasterPass a second trial, 7+ days after Journeyman100%

A rank trial is short — about half the set, at most 18 questions, weighted toward the ones you missed before. The waiting periods are the point: coming back to material after a few days is what makes it stick, so full credit is not something you can finish in one sitting.

Your score never goes down. A trial can only add credit, so a bad day costs you nothing but the wait. The main screen shows your current score and a How is this calculated? breakdown of exactly where it comes from.

Kinds of questions

Multiple choiceSometimes more than one answer is correct — select them all before submitting.
Fill in the blankType the missing term. Minor typos are forgiven.
MatchingPair each term with its definition.
Put in orderTap steps, or lines of code, into the right sequence.
NumericYou may type arithmetic instead of the answer — 12 * 5 works, and is worked out for you.
Trace the codeSay what a short program prints.
Write the codeWrite a real Python function. Press ▶ Run Tests as often as you like before submitting — it is free and does not count against you. return your answer rather than printing it.

Getting through it

  • Keys: 1–9 pick answers, Enter submits.
  • Read the feedback after a wrong answer. It explains why, and that question is coming back.
  • Leaving mid-set is fine — press ← Menu and pick Resume when you return.
  • Phones and tablets work. Code questions have ⇥ Indent and ⇤ Outdent buttons so you do not need a keyboard Tab.
  • After a set reaches Master it occasionally shows 🔁 Review due — a handful of questions to keep it fresh. Reviews do not change your grade.

Use a normal browser window. Your progress is saved in the browser and synced to D2L. Private or incognito browsing blocks that, and your work will be lost — the game will warn you in red if it cannot save.

Finish a set in one sitting where you can. If your character dies partway through, that run is not saved.
